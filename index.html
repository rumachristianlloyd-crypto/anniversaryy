My letter to you

Dear Baby,

Happy 1st Anniversary, My Baby.

We’ve been together for one year, baby. Looking back at everything we've shared this past year, I'm so grateful for every moment that brought us closer and for all the memories we've created together. We've been through so many things in this one year, so many happy moments, challenges, misunderstandings, laughs, and little moments that I'll always keep in my heart. And through all of it, I'm just really grateful that it's you I've experienced this year with.

Gusto ko po na malaman mo, baby, how beautiful and amazing you are to me. Not just because of how you look, but because of the kind of person you are. I love the way you care, kung paano mo ako alagaan sa mga oras na mahina ako, the way you understand me kahit sobrang gulo kong tao, the way you love, and sa mga maliliit na bagay na ginawa mo para sa akin that you probably don't realize mean so much to me. You have a way of making me feel loved and comfortable just by being yourself, baby, and I'm genuinely so lucky kasi mayroon akong ikaw.

Thank you so, so, so, so much for everything po, baby. Thank you for staying with me kahit hindi ko pa alam what my plan is para sa buhay ko, for understanding me because sometimes I hurt your feelings, for being patient with me, and for choosing us even during the moments when things weren't easy. Thank you for all the love, comfort, memories, conversations, laughs, and time you've given me. Thank you so much po, baby, kasi naniniwala ka sa akin and for being someone I can always find comfort in. I know I've made mistakes throughout our relationship, and I'm truly thankful that you didn't give up on me or sa atin dalawa. I'll always appreciate the fact that you stayed and continued to choose me.

And I want to promise you that I'll keep trying to become a better partner for you, baby. I promise to keep learning from my mistakes, to communicate better, to listen to you, to understand you, and to take care of your heart as much as I can. I promise that I won't take what we have for granted. And baby, kapag dumating yung araw na nag succeed ako and I'm finally able to give you the life we've always talked about, I promise gagawin ko lahat ng makakaya ko to make that dream a reality for us. I want to build that future with you, the future we've talked about, the places we want to see, the things we want to experience, and the peaceful life we dream of having together. I may not have everything right now, but I'm going to keep working toward it, baby.

I love you more than I can properly put into words, baby. I love you for who you are, for the memories we've made, and for everything that makes you, you. Ikaw po yung taong gusto ko maka-sama, the person I want to make more memories with, and the person I genuinely want a future with. One year with you is something I'll always be grateful for, but ayaw ko na mag stop lang sa one year. I want more months, more years, more adventures, more laughs, more dates, more simple moments, and more life with you.

Happy 1st anniversary, my baby. Thank you for making this past year so special and for being such an important part of my life. I'll keep choosing you, loving you, and doing my best to make you feel loved and secure with me. I love you so, so much, baby, more than you know, more than I can say, and I hope I get to show you that love for many, many more years. 

I love you very, very much, baby

And if I had to choose again,
I'd still choose you. Always.
